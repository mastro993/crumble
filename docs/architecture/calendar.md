# Calendar and Agenda

Part of the [native architecture](native.md): the Calendar views and the Agenda.

## Calendar

Terms such as Calendar view, Period, and Focused date are defined in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md).

- The Calendar page shows one Period in a Week, Month, or Year Calendar view. It opens on today in the Calendar view last picked, with the switcher or by clicking a Month day or a Year month, which is saved in `app_state.calendar_view` and starts as Month view. The Focused date stays in memory while the app runs and is not persisted.
- On the Calendar route the header centers Previous, the Period title, and Next on the window, and holds a segmented Week | Month | Year switcher at its trailing end. A Today button sits at its leading end, after the header controls, only while the shown Period does not contain today; the Today shortcut works either way.
- `Cmd-T` jumps to today and `Cmd-←` / `Cmd-→` step one Period (`Ctrl` elsewhere). These shortcuts are handled only while the Calendar page is open.
- Week view is an hourly grid from 00:00 to 24:00 that scrolls on its own. It opens scrolled to 08:00 and keeps its scroll position while the app runs. In the week that holds today, a red line marks the current time across every day, thicker on today and starting there from a dot, with the time in red in the hour column in place of an hour label it would overlap. It is drawn over the Events and moves at the start of each minute. Month view stretches 4–6 week rows to fill the page and mutes days from neighbouring months. Clicking a day opens its week in Week view; clicking an Event opens its page instead. Year view shows twelve months as rounded cards in a 4×3 grid. Each card always has six week rows, so the cards in a grid row end level, and the grid stays at most 1200 pt wide. The current month's name is bold. Clicking anywhere on a month card opens that month in Month view. Clickable days and month cards show a pointer cursor.
- Week and Month grids sit in a bordered frame with 12 pt rounded corners (`SURFACE_RADIUS`). Shaded cells in the corners follow the curve, including while the Week hours scroll.
- Today is circled in the accent color. Weekends (Saturday and Sunday) are shaded in every Calendar view. In Year view, adjacent weekend columns share one rounded strip.
- Today is read from the local clock on every render, so a window left open past midnight updates on its next redraw.
- Week and Month views draw the Events of the dates on screen, from every Calendar that is not Disabled and whose Account is neither Disabled nor Hidden; Year view draws none. Each Event takes its Account's color (see Events), so every Calendar of an Account shares it. The Events are read off the main thread again when the Period, the Calendar view, the First day of week, a Disabled or Hidden choice, or EventKit changes. A failed read is logged and the last Events stay.
- In Week view, timed Events are blocks placed by time over their day's column, at least 22 minutes tall so short ones stay clickable, with 6 pt after each block and 4 pt between one block and the next below it. Overlapping Events share the column side by side. Blocks under 45 minutes show only their title. An Event crossing midnight is drawn on each day it touches, and one that ends at midnight stays on the day before. All-day Events sit in a strip between the day names and the hours, two per day before "+N more".
- In Month view, each day lists its all-day Events as tinted bars, then its timed Events by start as a colored dot, the start time, and the title. A day shows three before "+N more".
- Resting the pointer on an Event for 500 ms shows its details card beside it: the title in up to two lines, when it happens, the description flattened into up to three lines, each cut with an ellipsis, the first three participants before "+N more", and a full-width Join button named after the meeting service when it has a meeting link. The card opens to the right of the Event with their top edges level, flips to the left when the window has no room on the right, and stays at least 8 pt inside the window, so it never covers its Event. It closes 150 ms after the pointer leaves both the Event and the card, so the pointer can cross the gap to reach its Join button. Crumble draws the card itself instead of using GPUI Kit's `HoverCard` (see Cards).
- Clicking an Event, or pressing Enter or Space while it has focus, opens its page, and Back returns to the Calendar page. Tab reaches each Event. The Calendar button stays selected in the rail. The page shows the Event's Note in the Note detail view (see Notes). Its header holds a breadcrumb, "Calendar › title", whose Calendar opens the Calendar page and whose title truncates, then on the right a description button, a participants button showing how many were invited, and a Join button named after the meeting service, then the Note actions button once the Event has a Note. Each button shows only when the Event has what it opens. The description and participants buttons open a 360 pt wide card 16 pt below them, right-aligned with them, which scrolls past 420 pt and closes when clicked away from or with Escape (see Cards). The participants box lists everyone. In the description box, web links and linked text are underlined in the theme's link color and open in the browser when clicked; the hover card shows linked text without its address. The header shows the Event as it was when it was opened, or as last read while it is still in the Calendar page's dates, so an EventKit change shows up there. A moved Event is found again by its series UID and occurrence, unless more than one Event in those dates has them, as when one Event shows in two Calendars.
- Each participant shows an icon for their answer (accepted, maybe, declined, delegated, or awaiting), with the name struck through when they declined, and "Organizer" or "You" after the name. Hovering the row names the answer. Crumble never changes Events.

## Agenda

Agenda page, Agenda list, and In progress are defined in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md).

- The Agenda page lists every Event that starts by the end of today, including today's Events still to come. It uses the same Calendars as the Calendar page: none that is Disabled or whose Account is Disabled or Hidden. It also lists every Quick note listed in the days it has read, whatever is Disabled or Hidden. The header shows no title; it holds a New note button on the left, left out without calendar access, and, while the selected row has a Note, the Note actions button on the right (see Notes). New note makes a Quick note now, selects its row, and focuses its Memo; if it cannot, the error is logged and a "Couldn't make a note" toast shows.
- The Agenda list is the page's sidebar (see Shell), a column running from the panel's leading edge to its bottom, rounded to the panel's leading corners, with a hairline on its trailing edge. Its surface is a shade between the sidebar color and the page's. Its first day header sits 16 pt below the panel's top, the same space as between days. The list stays empty until its first read finishes, then its rows fade in over 280 ms while rising 8 pt into place, or appear at once when the system asks for reduced motion. A first read that fails shows the list as it is. Rows scrolled past the list's top fade out over its top 32 pt instead of being cut off, the fade rounded to the column's top-leading corner; the fade grows in over the first 32 pt scrolled, so the list at rest is not dimmed. It starts 320 pt wide, resizes, and hides with the sidebar toggle. The content area beside it shows the selected row's Note in the Note detail view, and is empty while no row is selected.
- Days run newest first under a header: "Today", "Yesterday", then the date, with the year only outside the current year. Each Event is listed once, under the day it started, except that In progress Events sit under Today whatever day they started. Today lists its upcoming Events first, latest first, then the In progress ones; then come Today's all-day Events and its ended Events, newest first. Now is marked between them. When the last of the In progress Events is there, its card marks it: tinted in the accent color, filled from its leading edge as far as the Event has gone, and saying what is left of it, such as "48m left", at its trailing end. Otherwise the Now line marks it: a thin line in the accent color across the full width of the list, starting from a dot level with the rows' bars. The list is built again each minute, so the fill and the time left keep up and an Event moves below now as it starts. Other days list their all-day Events first, then the rest newest first. A Quick note sits under the day of its Recording's start, else the day it was made, at that time, among the timed Events; a day with only Quick notes still gets its header.
- An Event row shows a bar in its Account's color, the title on one line, and the time: "09:00 – 10:30", "All day", or both dates for an Event across days, as the details card writes it. A Quick note row shows a neutral bar, its title or "Quick note", and the time it is listed at.
- Clicking a row, or pressing Enter or Space while it has focus, selects it; Tab reaches each row. The selected row is filled as a selected sidebar row is, and the others fill on hover. The selection stays while the app runs and when the list is built again: an Event that moved is found again by its series UID and occurrence, and a row that is gone clears it, as losing calendar access does.
- The list reads the last 30 days first, then 30 more days back at a time while it holds fewer than 40 rows or is scrolled within 20 rows of its end, skipping empty stretches. It stops five years back with "End of Agenda", or "No past Events" when it found none. Each read runs off the main thread, in pieces of at most a year, since EventKit searches at most four years at once. The read that covers today also keeps timed Events that started before it and are still In progress. A failed read is logged and the last Events stay; it is not tried again until EventKit reports a change or a different read is wanted.
- The Agenda reads nothing until it is first opened. Its Events and scroll position stay in memory while the app runs. The days already read, never more than five years back, are read again when the day changes, when a Disabled or Hidden choice changes, or after EventKit reports a change. Quick notes are read from `crates/notes` each time the rows are built; a failed read is logged and the last Quick notes stay. While the Agenda is open, it updates on each minute, so an Event joins the list when it starts and leaves In progress when it ends.
- Once the list is scrolled away from its top, a round button with an up arrow floats centered near the column's top. Clicking it scrolls the list back to the top over 300 ms, or at once when macOS asks for reduced motion; scrolling by hand stops it. The button goes away once the list is at its top.
- Without calendar access the column says to connect calendars from the Calendars button.
